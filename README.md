# 🌾 Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

**Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis** is a data analysis project that studies agricultural performance across different farming seasons.

The project analyzes **4,000 agricultural records** to understand patterns and variations in crop yield, profitability, water usage, environmental conditions, and disease/pest risk.

The analysis focuses mainly on the **Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid** seasons.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze agricultural performance across different seasons.
* Compare crop yield and profitability.
* Study water usage and water-use efficiency.
* Analyze environmental factors such as rainfall and soil moisture.
* Examine disease and pest risk.
* Compare agricultural performance across different states.
* Identify useful patterns and trends from the dataset.
* Provide evidence-based recommendations for better agricultural planning.

---

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains **4,000 agricultural records** with **28 columns**.

The dataset includes information related to:

* Season
* Crop
* State
* Yield
* Profit
* Water usage
* Rainfall
* Soil moisture
* Disease/Pest risk
* Irrigation methods
* Production costs
* Revenue
* Other agricultural and environmental factors

## 🔍 Methodology

The project follows these major steps:

1. **Data Loading**

   * Load the agricultural dataset using Pandas.

2. **Data Inspection**

   * Examine dataset structure, columns, data types, and missing values.

3. **Data Preprocessing**

   * Identify missing values.
   * Apply median imputation where required.
   * Verify the cleaned dataset.

4. **Exploratory Data Analysis**

   * Analyze seasonal performance.
   * Compare crop-wise performance.
   * Study irrigation and water usage.
   * Analyze environmental factors.
   * Compare state-wise performance.
   * Examine economic performance.

5. **Data Visualization**

   * Create charts and plots to identify patterns and trends.

6. **Findings and Recommendations**

   * Summarize important observations.
   * Provide evidence-based recommendations.

---

## 💡 Key Findings

* Agricultural yield varies across different seasons.
* **Kharif** shows stronger overall performance in the available dataset.
* **Zaid** shows comparatively lower average yield and profitability.
* Water usage differs across seasons and irrigation methods.
* Crop profitability varies significantly between crops.
* Environmental factors show relationships with agricultural outcomes.
* Agricultural performance also varies across states.
* Economic outcomes depend on factors such as production costs and revenue.
